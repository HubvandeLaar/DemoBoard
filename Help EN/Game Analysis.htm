Game Analysis

Analysis of the active game.

The analysis starts with a form requesting the level of the players. Possible levels are Step 1, Step 2 and Step 3.

The analysis detects mistakes and omissions in the game. When such are found, the comment of the move is adapted with text and diagram, and possibly a new variation is added with the correct move.

The following mistakes and omissions are detected:

Step 1

  • Incorrect initial position
  • Same ccolor played twice
  • Invalid castling
  • In check after move
  • Is invalid move
  • Missed mate in one
  • Missed to capture an uncovered piece
  • Forgot to cover an own uncovered piece
  • Missed a two-fold attack
  • Forgot to cover a two-fold attacked piece
  • Missed a profiable exchange
  • Unprofitable capture. Piece was attacked, but also defended
  • UnProfitable exchange
  • Opponent can capture an isufficiently defended piece

Step 2

  • Opponent can checkmate
  • Missed checkmate in two
  • Missed pinning
  • Missed double attack
  • Missed elimination of defence
  • Missed discoverd attack

Step 3

  • Missed to finish the opening
  • Missed a discovered check
  • Missed a double check
  • Missed to attack a pinned piece
  • Missed a pinned piece is a bad defender
  • Missed to defend against pinning
