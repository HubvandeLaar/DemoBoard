Export Games to PDF

Exports games into a PDF document.

At the beginning, a selection form is presented with all games in the file. Clicking a game, selects the game for export. Use Ctrl+A to select all games. Depending whether the game was analysed (using Game\Analyse Game) and a 'Annotator'-tag with 'DemoBoard' was added, 'Position before:' or 'Position after' is printed beneath every diagram.

The PDF can be printed and handed out to students as feedback upon their games.
